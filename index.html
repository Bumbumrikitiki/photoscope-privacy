Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

PhotoScope is a photo analysis app. This policy explains what information the app processes and how it is handled.

Data controller

The controller for PhotoScope is ROBERT SOWIŃSKI UNIVOLT, ul. Marcina Kasprzaka 29c-59, 01-234 Warszawa, Poland. For privacy questions or requests, contact gridly.electricalchecker.support@gmail.com. Developer website: bumbumrikitiki.github.io/gridly.

Photo analysis

PhotoScope analyzes photos selected or captured by you. It reads image dimensions, colors, exposure and sharpness statistics, metadata embedded in the file, and the number and position of detected faces. Analysis takes place on your device. PhotoScope does not upload your photo to its own servers or keep a photo history.

Metadata and location

If present in the selected file, PhotoScope may display EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC and other supported image metadata, including camera settings, capture date and GPS coordinates. This information is read locally and is not transmitted by PhotoScope. Metadata can be changed or removed by the camera or other software before you select the file. PhotoScope does not add location information to your photos.

Face detection

On Android, Google ML Kit detects face boxes on-device so PhotoScope can report their count and position. The app does not identify people, recognize who someone is, estimate age or gender, or judge appearance. Face data is not uploaded or retained by PhotoScope.

Advertising and consent

PhotoScope is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and other information as described in Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for consent choices before ads are requested. An ad may appear while a photo is selected and a full-screen ad may be attempted before the report. Ads do not change analysis results. If an ad is unavailable, the report remains accessible.

Sharing

PhotoScope can create a report card containing the selected photo and summary information. It is shared only when you choose a destination through the system share interface. PhotoScope does not send the card on its own.

Legal bases, retention and transfers

PhotoScope processes the selected image locally to provide the report you request. The developer does not receive or retain the image or report. Google AdMob processes advertising data under its own terms and privacy disclosures; where applicable, consent is requested through UMP. Google may process data on servers outside your country under its published safeguards and terms.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. For requests concerning the app or this policy, contact the controller at gridly.electricalchecker.support@gmail.com. For data processed by Google for advertising, use Google's privacy tools and contact channels.

Retention and children

PhotoScope does not require an account and does not keep a server-side photo or analysis history. The app is a general photo utility and is not directed to children under 13.
